# CivicGuard Autopilot — Limitations & Operational Boundaries

This document provides an honest, rigorous specification of the operational boundaries and technical constraints of **CivicGuard Autopilot**. In accordance with strict engineering integrity standards, we make no unsubstantiated claims regarding mathematical guarantees or production deployment readiness.

---

## 1. Remediation Proof: Operational Evidence Certificate, Not Formal Mathematical Proof

- **What it is:** The **Remediation Proof** is an immutable, versioned, cryptographic digest snapshot combining time-bounded telemetry evidence, heuristic root-cause ranking subscores, reverse-dependency cascade calculations, deterministic policy evaluation results, guardrail baselines, and certified rollback procedures.
- **What it is NOT:** It is **not a formal mathematical safety proof** (such as TLA+, Coq, or Isabelle formal verification). It cannot mathematically guarantee that an autonomous infrastructure mutation has zero unforeseen side effects in complex, non-deterministic distributed environments.
- **Zero Full-Rollout Regressions is an Evaluation Target:** Zero full-rollout regressions represents an empirical evaluation objective validated against tested benchmark scenarios, never a mathematically inviolable invariant.

---

## 2. Root-Cause Ranking: Heuristic Correlation, Not Calibrated Causal Probability

- **Ranking Strength Score:** Candidate scores (e.g. 87/100) are generated through an explainable multi-signal heuristic weighting:
  $$\text{RCA} = 0.25 \, \text{Temporal} + 0.20 \, \text{Magnitude} + 0.20 \, \text{Downstream} + 0.15 \, \text{Trace} + 0.10 \, \text{Log} + 0.10 \, \text{Topology}$$
- **Not a Calibrated Bayesian Probability:** A score of 87 does **not** signify an 87% calibrated statistical likelihood or causal certainty. It represents relative heuristic rank strength among observed anomalous services.
- **Intermediary Node Ambiguity:** In rapid failure cascades occurring within sub-second intervals below telemetry sampling resolution, intermediate proxies or gateways may occasionally show simultaneous anomaly onsets.

---

## 3. Simulation Mode vs. Kubernetes Lab Integration

- **Default Simulation Mode:** By default, CivicGuard runs entirely locally with zero cloud credentials, Docker, or Kubernetes prerequisites. The simulator maintains an internal discrete-event state model (traffic allocation splits, pod replicas, application image revisions).
- **Kubernetes Lab Adapter (`civicguard-lab`):**
  - Strictly restricted to the isolated `civicguard-lab` namespace.
  - Requires explicit opt-in: `CIVICGUARD_MODE=k8s_lab` and `CIVICGUARD_REAL_ACTIONS=true`.
  - Enforces least-privilege RBAC. Never uses `cluster-admin`.
  - Rejects arbitrary shell commands, raw scripts, or unsanctioned namespaces.
  - Dry-run verification is enabled by default to prevent unauthorized cluster mutation.

---

## 4. Audit Log Integrity & Trust Model

- **Tamper-Evident, Not Tamper-Proof:** The SQLite audit ledger uses an HMAC-SHA-256 cryptographic hash chain (`previousHash` $\to$ `eventHash`).
- **Trust Assumptions:** Any post-facto modification of historical events or sequence numbers is immediately flagged by `/api/audit/verify`. However, because the database resides on the local host filesystem, an adversary possessing root access to the underlying storage volume and the signing key could recalculate the entire chain. In high-assurance production deployments, audit records should be continuously streamed to immutable write-once-read-many (WORM) cloud ledgers (e.g., AWS CloudTrail, Sigstore, or append-only ledger databases).

---

## 5. Statistical Power & Canary Measurement Duration

- **Sample Size Gating:** Canary evaluation requires at least 3 consecutive sampling windows with sufficient request volume. Under extremely low traffic (< 10 requests per window), statistical power is limited, and the engine flags `INSUFFICIENT_TELEMETRY` rather than optimistically assuming healthy status.
- **Hysteresis Windows:** Incident recovery requires 3 consecutive clean observation windows to prevent flapping or false recovery declarations.

---

## 6. Synthetic Public-Service Data Only

- All citizen records, entitlement calculations, identity verifications, and financial disbursement events generated by the synthetic simulator are 100% synthetic. No real citizen Personally Identifiable Information (PII), Aadhaar numbers, Social Security numbers, or real banking rails are accessed or stored.
